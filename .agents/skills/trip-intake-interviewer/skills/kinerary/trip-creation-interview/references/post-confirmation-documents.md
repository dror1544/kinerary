# Supplemental documents after confirmation

When an organizer supplies vouchers, tickets, or booking documents after the intake is confirmed:

1. Treat them as local intake supplements, not as a direction to provision anything.
2. Extract text using an appropriate document workflow, then classify each item with a short label and category: flight, accommodation, rental car, activity, insurance, or voucher.
3. Record operational facts only: supplier, date/time, route or location, vehicle or booking type, included items, and pickup/check-in requirements.
4. Keep booking references, ticket numbers, payment-card fragments, email addresses, and similar identifiers out of chat summaries. Retain source documents locally.
5. Store supplemental tags separately from the confirmed request; do not overwrite the confirmation or imply that a website, bot, or upload has occurred.

## Hebrew agent identity clarification

If an organizer wants the assistant to address each traveler in the grammatical form appropriate to them, record that as an addressing preference. Still obtain one explicit grammatical form for the assistant's self-reference; in Hebrew this cannot be left ambiguous.
