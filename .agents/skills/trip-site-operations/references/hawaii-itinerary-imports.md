# Hawaii itinerary imports

Condense organizer itinerary documents into daily activity rows for the trip site.

## Recommended row shape
- One `type: attraction` row per day per phase.
- `name` should be the concise day label the organizer can scan quickly.
- `notes` should carry the operational detail: key stops, reservations, pacing, and cautions.

## Example day headlines
- `12.8 — הגעה ל־Waikiki`
- `13.8 — Diamond Head + Waikiki / Ala Moana`
- `18.8 — Road to Hana + Black Sand Beach`
- `20.8 — בוקר רגוע + West Maui + טיסת ערב`

## What belongs in notes
- Advance-booking requirements: Diamond Head, Kualoa Ranch, USS Arizona, Waiʻānapanapa, Haleakalā sunrise.
- "Bonus only" stops that should not become separate must-do commitments.
- Safety and pacing notes: leave early, avoid overpacking a day, do not combine major long-drive days, distinguish view-only beaches from calm swim beaches.
- Flight-day handling: preserve calm-morning / packing / return-car guidance inside the same day entry as the verified evening flight.

## Conflict resolution rule
If an itinerary document conflicts with the trip's verified flights or transfer dates, trust the verified trip logistics and rewrite the daily attraction rows to match them. Example: move a stale `21.8 morning + flight` Maui note into the verified `20.8` Maui flight day instead of creating a contradictory extra day.
