# DEN rental-car return and family drop-off notes

Useful for same-day Denver departure support when the family wants to drop passengers first, then return a rental car.

## Family drop-off for United departures
- Denver International Airport has **one main terminal**: **Jeppesen Terminal**.
- For a Google Maps destination that gets the family closest to check-in / bag drop, use:
  - `Denver International Airport – Jeppesen Terminal, Departures`
  - address fallback: `8500 Peña Blvd, Denver, CO 80249`
- At DEN, Concourses **B** and **C** require the intra-airport train after security; only some A gates are walkable.

## Budget DEN rental return
Verified from Budget's DEN location page:
- Street address: **24050 E 78th Ave, Denver, CO 80249**
- Return directions snippet: from Peña Blvd take the **Rental Car** exit, go left, then right onto **78th Ave**, then proceed to the Budget lot.
- Budget states the lot is on airport property and approximately **5–6 minutes from the terminal**.

## Returning from Budget back to the terminal
Budget DEN page says:
- go to the **baggage claim area (Level 5)**
- step outside for the **Budget shuttle on Island 4**

## Practical recommendation
If one adult is returning the rental car, the low-stress flow is usually:
1. drop the family at **Jeppesen Terminal / Departures** with the bags
2. they handle check-in, bag drop, and security
3. driver returns the car to **Budget, 24050 E 78th Ave**
4. driver takes the Budget shuttle back to the terminal alone

This avoids making the whole family do the rental-return + shuttle loop.
