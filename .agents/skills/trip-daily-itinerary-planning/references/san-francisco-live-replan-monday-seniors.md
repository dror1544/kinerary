# San Francisco live replan: Monday with seniors, cable car, and compressed sights

Use case: the user revised the next-day San Francisco plan after a late night. They wanted to keep the original Monday anchors but add/modify:
- 08:00 hotel departure
- Joe & The Juice breakfast near hotel
- ride the Cable Car toward Chinatown
- keep Chinatown, Lombard Street, and Pier 39 / sea lions
- grandparents return to hotel to rest around 13:00
- dinner remains Osha Thai at 19:30

## Final working structure

- 08:00 leave hotel
- 08:05-08:45 Joe & The Juice near hotel
- 08:45-09:30 walk to Cable Car and ride toward Chinatown
- 09:30-10:45 Chinatown: stroll, stalls, bakery, snacks
- 10:45-11:15 Uber/car to Lombard Street (not on foot for seniors)
- 11:15-11:45 Lombard Street
- 11:45-12:15 Uber/car to Pier 39
- 12:15-13:00 Pier 39 + sea lions together
- 13:00 grandparents return to hotel
- 13:00-15:00 others continue by energy: more Pier 39 / North Beach / Ferry Building / Embarcadero / Union Square
- 17:30 latest everyone back at hotel
- 18:45-19:00 leave for Osha Thai / Embarcadero
- 19:30 Osha Thai dinner

## Planning lesson

When the user says “add what I said to the original Monday plan,” do not replace the original anchors. Preserve the existing musts and integrate the new constraint. In this case the error was dropping Pier 39 and Lombard when adding Cable Car + Chinatown + grandparents rest.

## Mobility/route lesson

Chinatown to Lombard and Lombard to Pier 39 are short by car but not senior-friendly walks:
- Chinatown → Lombard: ~5-7 min drive; budget 15-20 min with Uber/loading
- Lombard → Pier 39: ~5-7 min drive; budget ~15 min with Uber/loading
- Pier 39 → hotel: ~6-8 min drive; budget 15-20 min

Use Uber/car for these segments when grandparents are present, because San Francisco hills and curbs make “short” walks deceptively tiring.

## Style lesson

If the user asks a pointed logistics question like “is this on foot?” or “how long from place to place?”, answer the specific transport question first, then update the itinerary only if requested. Avoid broad re-summarizing when the user is tuning one segment.
