# Mapping confirmed interview output into the profile handoff

The control-plane interview currently emits schema-v2 answers keyed by question ID. Keep that canonical answer store in its immutable `intake_versions` record. Generate this separate normalized `trip_assistant_profile_input` contract from the confirmed version; do not copy raw question-keyed answers into the portable profile payload.

Only render after the organizer confirms the interview recap. Preserve `source.intake_version_ref`, `source.intake_schema_version`, `source.confirmed_at`, and `source.intake_digest`.

## Identity and trip fields

- `profile.name`: a new lowercase Hermes profile name; never clone an organizer's name by default.
- `trip.id`, title, dates, timezone, canonical site URL, and site connection name: provisioning output plus confirmed trip basics.
- `assistant.name`: `bot_name`.
- `assistant.gender`: `bot_gender`; ask explicitly for Hebrew trips.
- `assistant.tone`: `bot_tone`.
- `assistant.proactive`: `bot_proactive`, using the platform's default times when the interview collected only on/off choices.
- `organizer.person_ref`: resolve `organizer_identity` to an opaque person reference in the generated roster. Do not guess when matching fails.
- Provider-specific identities and Telegram user IDs belong in protected runtime binding records, not this portable input.

## Group-safe projection

Put only information suitable to repeat aloud to the whole family in `interview.group_safe`:

- `trip_pace` may become a group-safe pace preference.
- general interests and recommendation style may be group-safe when they do not identify a private need;
- communication style requested for the whole group may be group-safe;
- a standing instruction is group-safe only when the organizer explicitly approved it for the family audience.

## Organizer-private projection

Put the following in `interview.organizer_private` by default:

- `constraints` involving budget, family dynamics, mobility context, or conflict;
- every `bot_limits` entry;
- topics to avoid, private alert rules, organizer goals, and planning concerns;
- any preference that explains or identifies a participant's private behavior.

Never promote an item to group-safe merely because it sounds harmless. The organizer must explicitly approve the wider visibility.

## Participant needs

Map `dietary` plus `dietary_scope` to `participant_needs` when a named traveler resolves to a participant username. Use:

- `visibility: organizer` by default;
- `severity: firm` for dietary restrictions;
- `severity: critical` for allergies;
- the bilingual text generated by the control-plane transformer.

Group-wide food policy can be a group-safe standing instruction only when the organizer approves it for the group. Medical and accessibility facts remain participant needs, not generic standing instructions.

## Data deliberately not copied into the profile handoff

Do not copy credentials, shared passwords, bot tokens, API keys, booking confirmation codes, enrollment links, chat/session history, gateway state, memories, logs, cron jobs, or runtime messaging stores. Operational trip data remains on the trip site and is read live.

## Required review before rendering

1. Organizer confirmed the recap.
2. Organizer identity resolves to a real participant.
3. Assistant name/gender/tone are explicit where needed.
4. Every preference is classified group-safe or organizer-private.
5. Every participant need has an explicit visibility and severity.
6. Canonical site URL and timezone are verified.
7. The handoff contains no secret-like keys.
