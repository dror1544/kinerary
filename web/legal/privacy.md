# Kinerary Privacy Policy

*Early-access version, 30 October 2026. This policy is under legal review and may be updated; we will tell
organizers before a material change. A Hebrew version will follow.*

**Who we are:** Kinerary is run by Dror Elul, a sole trader in Israel ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the
personal data described here.
**Contact:** privacy@ara-united.store. Write to this address for any privacy request.

## 1. What Kinerary is

An organizer signs up at app.ara-united.store, creates a trip, and answers questions from our Telegram bot
(@Kinerary_bot). We then build a private website for that trip and add an AI trip companion to the family's
Telegram group. To do this we process information about the organizer and about the people on the trip,
including children. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how long we keep it.

## 2. Who this policy covers

- **Organizers**: people who create an account and a trip.
- **Travellers**: the people the organizer lists on a trip (adults and children). Most travellers never sign up
  with us. The organizer gives us their details.
- **Group members**: people in a Telegram group where a Kinerary companion has been added.
- **Visitors** to app.ara-united.store and to a trip website.

## 3. Children

- Children do not create Kinerary accounts. Only an adult organizer (18 or over) can sign up.
- The organizer may tell us a child's name, age, family group and needs (for example dietary needs, allergies,
  medical or mobility needs). The organizer confirms they have the right to share this, as the child's parent
  or guardian or with the parent's or guardian's permission.
- The organizer may give a child a login to the trip website (a name and a password or PIN chosen by the
  organizer). That login only opens that one trip's site.
- Allergy and medical needs are visible only to the organizer by default. The organizer can choose to show a
  need to the group. If a need's type is not recognised, it is treated as organizer-only.
- The companion is instructed to treat the family group as semi-public, and to keep private needs, booking
  references and codes out of the group chat.
- We are adding a step that asks the organizer for explicit consent the first time a child or a medical or
  allergy need is entered on a trip. Until it is live, the organizer's confirmation above applies.
- Telegram's own minimum-age rules apply to anyone in a Telegram group.
- A parent or guardian can ask us to see, correct or delete a child's data (section 10).

## 5. Legal bases

- **Contract**: to provide the service you asked for (account, trip site, companion).
- **Legitimate interests**: security, preventing abuse, fixing faults and improving the product, including
  reading trip data and making de-identified route summaries, as section 7 describes (you can turn both
  off). We weigh these against your rights.
- **Consent**: where we ask for it, for example health-related needs, which the organizer provides on a
  traveller's behalf. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation**: where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations, you have the right to see the
information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete it. If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR
gives you the rights in section 10.

## 6. AI processing

- Messages, answers and uploaded documents are processed by AI models to understand them, read dates and
  bookings, plan the trip, and reply. AI output can be wrong; the organizer reviews plans before they reach
  the trip site.
- The models we use: Anthropic (Claude) for the interview and for reading documents; OpenAI, models reached
  through OpenRouter, and Ollama Cloud for some agents and as fallbacks when the main model is unavailable.
- We use these providers under their business or API terms.
- The companion does not make legal or other significant decisions about anyone. It suggests and answers.

## 7. Reading trip data to improve Kinerary, and routes for others

The Kinerary team may read trip data and conversations to evaluate and improve the product.
We read it; we do not change it except at the organizer's request.

This is on by default. An organizer can turn it off at any time in
Personal settings, or by writing to privacy@ara-united.store until that setting is available. Once it is
off, we look at that organizer's trips only to provide the service, keep it secure, fix a fault, or when the
law requires it.


**Routes for other travellers (planned).** We plan to turn a trip's route (the places visited, their order, and
how long was spent in each) into a de-identified summary, and to offer it as a suggestion to other people
planning a similar trip. A summary never includes names, photos, messages, booking details, or exact dates (the
month at most), and nothing else that identifies you or your family. We leave out routes that are too unusual
to stay anonymous. This will be on by default; an organizer can turn it off by writing to
privacy@ara-united.store, and a switch will come with the feature.

**Your photos stay with your trip.** We do not show your photos to anyone outside your trip. If we later invite
travellers to share a photo (for example of a place) as an example for other users, it will be a separate
choice, made photo by photo or trip by trip, and off unless you choose it.

## 8. Retention, trip end and archives

- **During the trip** your data is on the trip site and in the companion.
- **When a trip ends or is taken down**, we first make a backup: the trip site's data, the uploaded original
  documents, the companion's profile including its memory and last conversation state, and the trip's records
  in our control system. Then we delete the live site and the companion.
- **The archive** is kept while the organizer's account exists, and at most 3 years after the trip ends.
- **Next trip**: we plan to let the companion start an organizer's next trip with what it learned on the last
  one. When that is available, the organizer will be told what was carried and can keep it or start empty.
  Memory about people who are not on the new trip will not be carried, and one organizer's memory never
  reaches another organizer's trip.
- **Deleting**: the organizer can ask us to delete the companion's memory or a trip's archive at any time.
- Sign-in links and email tokens expire within hours to a day. Website sessions last 7 days.

**International transfers**: several providers are outside Israel. We transfer data to them only under the
safeguards the law allows, such as their data-processing terms.

## 10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- see the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a common format (JSON);
- correct it;
- delete it, including from archives;
- restrict or object to some processing;
- withdraw consent.

**How**: write to privacy@ara-united.store from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm it
is you. We answer within 30 days. There is no self-service delete or export button yet; we do it by hand.

A traveller who is not an organizer can ask us directly; we may involve the organizer. A parent or guardian
can act for a child.

You can complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or, in the EU or UK, to your local data
protection authority.

## 11. Cookies and browser storage

The portal (app.ara-united.store) sets only the cookies it needs to work. No advertising or analytics cookies.

| Name | What for | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `kit_session` | Keeps you signed in (not readable by page scripts) | 7 days |
| `kit_csrf` | Protects your actions against cross-site forgery | 7 days |
| `kit_gnonce` | Protects "Sign in with Google" against replay | 5 minutes |

The portal also keeps short-lived items in your browser tab's session storage (a draft trip form, a Telegram
Mini App marker); they are gone when you close the tab. A trip website keeps your sign-in token (30 days) and
your packing-list ticks in your browser's local storage.

Third-party content: the Google sign-in button and Google Fonts (Google), and inside Telegram the Telegram
Mini App script. These providers can see your IP address.

## 12. Telegram groups

- When an organizer adds the companion to a group, it can see messages in that group, as Telegram allows a
  bot to. It replies when it is addressed (by name, mention or reply) and as the group's settings allow.
- Messages not addressed to it may still be seen by the companion's model for context, and are part of the
  conversation state kept for up to 90 days. Our own counters keep no message text.
- Everyone in the group can see what the companion writes there. Do not post anything in a group that you do
  not want the group to see.
- Telegram's own privacy policy covers Telegram itself.

## 13. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and Telegram ids as digests. Access to production systems is limited to
the operator. Trip data is served through an allow-list, so a field is hidden unless it is meant to be shown.
No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law
requires.

## 14. Changes

We will post changes here with a new date, and tell organizers by email or Telegram before a material change
takes effect.
