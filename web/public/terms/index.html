2. What Kinerary is

Kinerary helps an organizer plan a group trip. It provides:

  • a web portal (app.ara-united.store) to sign up and create trips;
  • an interview in Telegram that collects the trip's details;
  • a private website for each trip;
  • an AI trip companion in the family's Telegram group.

Kinerary is in early access. Features change, may stop working, or may be removed. It is free during early access. If we ever start charging, we will tell you at least 30 days before, and nothing will be charged without your agreement.

3. Who can use it

  • Children can be travellers on a trip and can get a trip-site login, but only through an adult organizer who is their parent or guardian or has the parent's or guardian's permission.
  • Using Telegram also means following Telegram's terms.

4. Your account

We may suspend or close a trip or account that breaks these rules, and we will tell you why unless the law or safety prevents it.

7. AI output: check before you rely on it

The interview, document reading, planning and the companion use AI models. AI can be wrong: dates, times, opening hours, prices, travel rules, visa or health advice can be out of date or mistaken.

  • Always confirm important details (flights, bookings, entry requirements, medical or allergy information) with the airline, hotel, venue, embassy or a professional.
  • Kinerary does not book anything for you and is not a travel agent. It does not give medical, legal or immigration advice.
  • In an emergency, call local emergency services, not the companion.

  • You also give us permission to make de-identified route summaries from your trip and offer them to other users, as the Privacy Policy describes (section 7), unless the organizer has turned this off. A summary never identifies you or your family.

If you choose to share a photo of a place or a view with other users, you give us a free, non-exclusive permission to show it in Kinerary, with credit if you ask, until you withdraw it. Photos of people are never shown outside the trip; we leave out any photo in which we detect a person, and remove its location and camera details first.
